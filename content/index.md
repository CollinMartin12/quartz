This blog is about studies, news, and projects of a Data persurer. In this blog the topics mostly covered are #data-engineering, #data-science, #python, #sql, the and the life/studies of a Data Science & Engineering Student in #spain


Pursuing data for the past 3 years has built me a foundation in the world of tech. Starting just 3 years ago makes me believe that the best of yet to come and learn are in the future. Developing projects in all data related spaces #ml, #data-science , and #data-engineering provide me the level of knowledge of engineer i'd like to become. In this blog, i am posting in public, my journey to get there.

# About Me
I am Collin Martin, this blog was inspired by [Simon Spati ](https://www.ssp.sh/about/) as he, in a blog post of his mentioned that one can publicly set up a blog within minutes using obsidian notes. Not having used obsidian notes and seeing how perfectly organized his life seemed, I decided to give it a shot. I recommend checking out his content as it personally has changed my life for the better. 

Currently studying Data Science & Engineering in Madrid, Spain I have seen what the tech seen has to offer and what it takes to enter it. That frightens me. 'Given your experience and projects they have to let you in', wrong. The data scene is very competitive as often times teh people who we are competing with has gotten 3 masters degrees and 2 phd's. This is what drives me to do things differently and build a great foundation in the entire data pipeline. This is from Data-Engineering to MLOPs. Hopefully this blog becomes a way to show my skills and help me teach those who were or are in my shoes.

Cheers, Collin Martin
